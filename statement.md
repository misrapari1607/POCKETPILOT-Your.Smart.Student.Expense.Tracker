# Problem Statement:
    College students and hostel students often have difficulty managing their monthly money because they make frequent small expenses on food, transportation, shopping, entertainment, academic requirements, and other daily needs. Without proper tracking, students may lose track of where their money is being spent and may exceed their planned budget and may not be able save money which is a basic skill.
# Scoope of the Project:
    The Project focuses on developing a Python-based expense management system for students.The system can be used to:
        1.Record daily expenses.
        2.Record savings.
        3.Categorize expenses.
        4.Record Payment methods and notes.
        5.Select and associate expenses with specific dates.
        6.Calculate the total amount spent
        7.Display remaining budget.
        8.Analyze spending using charts such as pie charts.
        9.Monitor monthly financial activity.
    The project can be further extended in the future with features such as database storage, user authentication, automatic expense analysis, spending alerts, cloud synchronization, and AI-based financial recommendations. 
# Target Users:
    The primary target users of the project are:
    1.College Students
    2.Hostel Students
    3.Students living away from home
    4.Young users begginning to manage their personal finances
    The system is primarily designed to be simple and easy to use for users with basic computer knowledge.
